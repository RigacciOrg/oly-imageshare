Oly ImageShare imports photos from Olympus cameras over Wi-Fi.

The app connects to the camera's Wi-Fi access point and uses its HTTP
API to list images, display thumbnails, download full-resolution files
and (on supported cameras) delete them.

Features:

* Camera gallery — An adaptive thumbnail grid displaying all photos
  and videos on the memory card, newest first. Icons identify protected
  images (key), share-reserved images, videos and RAW (.ORF) files.
  Tap individual thumbnails to select or deselect images, or use bulk
  selection by page, for the entire gallery, or by protection or
  sharing status. Selected images can be downloaded or deleted.
  Progress is displayed and operations can be cancelled.

* Camera connection check — Verifies that the camera is reachable and
  displays its model and serial number, or a helpful error message if
  the connection fails.

* Settings — Configure the download destination (shared storage or a
  folder of your choice), thumbnail, button and label sizes, thumbnail
  cache retention, camera IP address and DCIM folder.

* About and Quit — View application information and exit the app.
  Quitting also cleans up the old thumbnail cache.
